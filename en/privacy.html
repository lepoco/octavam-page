Privacy policy

Octavam collects no data. The app needs no account, has no ads, analytics or tracking, and sends none of your data to any server.

  • MIDI files, settings and recent-song history are stored locally on your device only.
  • Bluetooth access is used only to talk to your MIDI instrument.
  • Microphone note detection is planned. When added, audio will be processed on-device only, never recorded or uploaded, and the app will ask for system permission.
  • The octavam.com site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages; we use no cookies or analytics. The host may process IP addresses per GitHub's policy.

Questions: GitHub Issues.
