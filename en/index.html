Before you play

Good to know.

Is Octavam really free?

Yes, every learning feature is free. Supporting the creator is optional.

How do I connect a piano to my Mac?
  1. Enable Bluetooth MIDI on your piano.
  2. On Mac, open Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio.
  3. Click the Bluetooth icon and connect your instrument.
  4. Select your piano in Octavam’s device panel.
How do I connect on iPad or iPhone?

Choose “Connect Bluetooth MIDI device” in the device panel. USB MIDI needs a suitable cable.

Do I need a particular piano?

Bluetooth MIDI or USB MIDI is enough. Bluetooth audio alone and microphone input are not supported.

Can I practice my own pieces?

Yes, open a .mid or .midi file. On Mac: ⇧⌘O.

Which languages does the app support?

Polish, English, German, French, Ukrainian and Russian.
