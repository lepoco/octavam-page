Before you play

Good to know.

Is Octavam really free?

Yes. Every learning feature is free. Octavam Maestro is optional support for the creator and does not unlock additional features.

How do I connect a piano to my Mac?
  1. Enable Bluetooth MIDI on your piano.
  2. On your Mac, open Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio.
  3. Click the Bluetooth icon and connect your instrument.
  4. Select it in Octavam’s device panel. The app will try to reconnect it automatically next time.
How do I connect on iPad or iPhone?

In Octavam’s device panel, tap “Connect Bluetooth MIDI device” and choose your instrument. For USB MIDI, you need a cable and, depending on your device, an adapter.

Do I need a particular piano?

MIDI support matters, rather than the brand. Check whether your instrument supports Bluetooth MIDI or USB MIDI. Bluetooth audio alone is not enough. Microphone note detection is not currently available.

Can I practice my own pieces?

Yes. Import a .mid or .midi file from the songs panel, or press ⇧⌘O on Mac. You can also start right away with the built-in lessons and Chopin course.

Which languages does the app support?

The app’s interface is available in English, Polish, German, French, Ukrainian and Russian.
